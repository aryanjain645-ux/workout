Privacy Policy.

RACKED · Last updated 2026-05-28

RACKED is a personal weightlifting log built and operated by a single user. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it lives.

What we collect

  • Workout data you enter: exercises, weights, reps, sets, dates.
  • Bodyweight entries you choose to log.
  • WHOOP data (if you connect WHOOP): recovery score, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep duration and timing, daily cycles, workout sessions (activity type, strain, heart rate, duration), and your basic profile, accessed via WHOOP's API under the scopes you grant.

Where it's stored

All workout and bodyweight data is stored in your own Supabase project, controlled by your credentials. RACKED is a static web app — there is no RACKED-operated backend that stores your data.

WHOOP data is fetched at view time and cached only in your device's local storage and service-worker cache to support offline use. It is not transmitted to any third party.

Who can see your data

Only you, plus anyone you explicitly share your Supabase URL and API key with. We do not sell, share, or have access to your data.

Third parties we rely on

  • Supabase — hosts your database. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Netlify — hosts the static app. See netlify.com/privacy.
  • WHOOP — source of recovery/sleep data, only if you connect it. See whoop.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used in the UI.

Revoking access

You can disconnect WHOOP at any time from your WHOOP app permissions page. You can wipe all RACKED data by clearing the relevant rows in your Supabase tables or clearing site data in your browser.
